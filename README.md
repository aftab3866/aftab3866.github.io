## Research Vision & Lab Strategy

I am actively seeking an **Assistant Professorship** with the objective of establishing an independent research group focused on multi-omics, computational biology, AI/ML, network-based biomedical research, and collaborative translational research.

### Multi-Omics Integration

Building scalable computational pipelines that integrate transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, and microbiomics into coherent and interpretable biological insights.

### AI/ML for Biomedicine

Developing and applying machine learning, deep learning, and explainable AI models to identify biomarkers, drug targets, and patient-stratification signatures.

### Network-Based Research

Leveraging protein–protein interaction and gene-regulatory networks to understand disease mechanisms and identify synthetic-lethal interactions and drug-repurposing opportunities.

### Translational Collaboration

Collaborating with clinicians, experimental biologists, and industry partners to translate computational discoveries into diagnostic and therapeutic strategies.
